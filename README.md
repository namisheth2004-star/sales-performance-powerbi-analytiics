## 📊 Key Highlights & Business Insights

### Page 1: Executive Financial Overview
* **Revenue Tracking:** Aggregated and visualized Total Orders, Total Revenue, and Average Order Value (AOV) to monitor overall business health.
* **Discount Impact:** Analyzed the relationship between Total Discounts and order volumes to identify margin leakages.
* **Trend Analysis:** Developed dynamic time-series charts to track sales performance and customer purchasing behavior.

### Page 2: Operations & Delivery Logistics
* **Bottleneck Identification:** Monitored Average Preparation Time against Rider Wait Time to pinpoint kitchen delays.
* **Fulfillment Efficiency:** Tracked Average Distance per trip to analyze driver dispatch efficiency and optimize delivery windows.
* **Service Level Agreements (SLAs):** Provided operational visibility to help reduce delivery friction and improve customer retention.
